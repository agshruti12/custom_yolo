Setup:
1. pip install ultralytics opencv-python pillow
2. Put your videos into raw_videos 

For each of your videos, do the following: 

python data_prep/detect_and_label.py --video raw_videos/<video_name> --output labelled_dataset --frame-skip 4

example: python data_prep/detect_and_label.py --video raw_videos/cyclar_data_5.mp4 --output labelled_dataset --frame-skip 4
